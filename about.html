About JDuuub Films

Professional photography and videography services based in Alabama.

The Story

I'm John Wyatt Mooney — a creator dedicated to capturing the energy and emotion behind machines and motion, as well as the special moments that make life memorable. I specialize in automotive, motorcycle, and action sports photography and videography, blending visual storytelling with dynamic sound design. I also offer portrait photography services for families, graduations, birthdays, sweet 16, and other milestone celebrations, bringing the same creative vision and technical expertise to every session.

What I Do

From high-octane automotive shoots to meaningful portrait sessions, I bring a cinematic approach to every project. Whether you're looking to showcase your vehicle in its best light or capture milestone moments like graduations, family gatherings, or special celebrations, I work closely with clients to understand their vision and deliver compelling visual content.

Equipment & Approach

Gear: Sony a7IV, DJI RS4 gimbal, DJI Mini 3 drone

I combine professional equipment with creative storytelling to produce photos and videos that stand out. Every project receives careful attention to composition, lighting, and post-production to ensure exceptional results.

Location & Availability

Based in Alabama and available for travel. I'm happy to work at your preferred location or suggest ideal spots based on your project needs.
